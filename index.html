Ling Liu

I am a second-year master's student in Data and Artificial Intelligence at Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris). I have been fortunate to work under the supervision of Prof. Maks Ovsjanikov at École Polytechnique (X), Prof. Amal Dev Parakkat at Télécom Paris (TP) and Prof. Yi Li at IIIS, Tsinghua University (THU), My research interests lie at the intersection of 3D Computer Vision and Computer Graphics.

TACO
